Who we are

One team, from the first call to the keys

Find Your Haven (FYH) is a boutique 360° real estate firm specialising in the search, assessment, transformation and management of homes across Catalonia's prime holiday areas. We are not an estate agency. We are not a developer. We are the team that works exclusively for you.

We guide every decision with a technical eye that goes beyond the listing: we understand a building from the inside before recommending it, and we coordinate every professional a purchase abroad requires — on your behalf.

Our model

Simple. And completely on your side

We don't charge you a fixed fee for our service. Instead, we work with a carefully selected network of local partners — estate agencies, architects, builders, lawyers, interior designers and property managers — and we are compensated through the professional relationships we maintain with them.

What this means for you: our coordination, our expertise and our time come at no additional cost. You pay for the property, the renovation, the services you choose — not for the layer of professional management that makes it all work.

No markups buried in quotes. No hidden referral fees added to your bill. A clean, transparent process — where you know exactly how it works.

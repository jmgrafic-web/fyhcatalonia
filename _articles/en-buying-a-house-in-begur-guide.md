Begur is one of the most sought-after towns on the Costa Brava, and also one of the most misunderstood: it isn't one place, but an old town, several coves and different developments with very different lives. This guide helps you understand what's in each area.

## The old town

Begur's centre is organised around the medieval castle, with cobbled streets and grand houses, among them those of the indianos who came back from the Americas in the late nineteenth century. It's the area with the most life all year round. The typical home is the village house, often with heritage value, which sometimes means limits on renovation and houses with little outdoor space.

## The coves and their villages

- **Sa Riera:** a family beach with a small cluster of apartments and houses right by the sand.
- **Sa Tuna:** a former fishermen's corner, one of the most photographed on the coast, with very limited stock.
- **Aiguablava and Fornells:** residential areas above coves, with villas and access to the sea.
- **Aiguafreda:** a small, secluded cove surrounded by pines.

## Property types

- **Village houses and grand town houses** in the old town.
- **Villas with a pool and views** in the hillside developments, the option families who want space look for most.
- **Apartments** near the coves, the most accessible entry to the area.

The usual purchase range in the premium part of the Costa Brava is between €400,000 and €1.2 million, depending on the type of property and proximity to the sea.

## What to check before you make an offer

- **The Coastal Law.** Near the sea, the protection easement affects buildings in the first metres from the shoreline. Check whether the property or its plot is affected, because it limits renovations and extensions.
- **Mains drainage or septic tank.** Not every development is connected to the network, and the condition of the septic tank is a common source of problems and costs.
- **Water and drought.** During droughts, restrictions can apply to filling pools and to irrigation. Find out the municipality's situation.
- **The condition of the house and the paperwork,** including the occupancy licence and the [habitability certificate]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/blog/habitability-certificate-catalonia/), which a [technical due diligence]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/blog/technical-due-diligence-property-explained/) reviews.
- **Holiday letting.** Each municipality sets its own licensing policy; check it before buying with that in mind, as we explain in [holiday letting and licensing in Catalonia]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/blog/vacation-rental-catalonia-licence/).

## Life year-round

Begur stays considerably more active outside summer than other villages on the coast, though the pace changes compared with July and August. If you're considering settling, our guide to [living year-round on the Costa Brava]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/blog/living-year-round-costa-brava/) covers healthcare, schools and connections. Girona airport is a little under an hour away and Barcelona under two.

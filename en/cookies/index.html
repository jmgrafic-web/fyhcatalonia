Cookie policy

Last updated: November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or the pages you have viewed.

Cookies used on this website

At present, findyourhaven.com does not set any first- or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising or personalisation. That is why we do not show a cookie banner or ask for your consent.

TypeWhat they are forOn this website
TechnicalMaking the website workNot used
AnalyticsMeasuring visits and how the site is usedNot used
AdvertisingShowing ads based on your interestsNot used

Third-party services

To display the website, Google Fonts typefaces and images hosted by third parties (such as Unsplash or Pexels) are loaded, and forms are sent through Formspree. These providers may receive your IP address in order to serve the content; we do not use them to track your browsing.

Changes to this policy

If we add analytics or other tools that use cookies in the future, we will update this policy, list each cookie and ask for your consent before setting them.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings: Chrome · Firefox · Safari · Edge.

If you have any questions, write to us at hello@findyourhaven.com.

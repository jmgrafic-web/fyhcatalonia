Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

Find Your Haven. You can write to us at hello@findyourhaven.com.

What we collect and why

What you give us in the contact form (name, email, optional phone, the type of enquiry and your message) and the email you use to request the free guide. We use it to answer your enquiry and send you the guide. If you subscribe, we may also send you news from Find Your Haven; you can unsubscribe at any time.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. Our forms are handled by Formspree, a provider based outside the European Economic Area that acts on our behalf. We only share your data with one of our partners (for example a lawyer or a builder) if you authorise it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to deal with your enquiry and, after that, only while responsibilities may still arise. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, ask for portability and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hello@findyourhaven.com. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Cookies and third-party services

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. To display the site, Google Fonts typefaces and images hosted by third parties (such as Unsplash or Pexels) are loaded, so your IP address may reach those servers.
